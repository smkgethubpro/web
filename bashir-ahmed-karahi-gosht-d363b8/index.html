About Us

Located in the heart of Multan, Bashir Ahmed Karahi Gosht serves traditional Punjabi cuisine prepared with pure desi ghee. Our menu features signature chicken karahi, mutton karahi, butter chicken, kebabs, fresh fish and a variety of sides. Enjoy your meal in our rooftop dining area, air-conditioned hall, or separate male/female seating sections. We also offer takeaway and stay open late into the night.
