Welcome

Serving authentic Punjabi flavors since 1998

Experience the true taste of Punjab with our signature Desi Chicken Karahi, slow-cooked in pure desi ghee with aromatic spices.

Located in the heart of Multan, we offer a warm family atmosphere with separate seating areas and ample parking.

  • Authentic Taste — Traditional recipes passed down through generations
  • Reasonable Prices — Quality desi food at affordable rates
  • Separate Seating — Dedicated family and gents sections
  • Parking Available — Convenient parking space for customers
